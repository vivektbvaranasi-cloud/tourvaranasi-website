Where to Stay in Varanasi: Ghats, Assi or Cantonment?

Where to stay in Varanasi for a private trip: the trade-offs between river atmosphere, hotel character, walking and straightforward vehicle access.

Quick answer

There is no single best area to stay in Varanasi. Riverfront hotels give the strongest atmosphere, while Nadesar and Cantonment are usually easier for vehicles. For BrijRama Palace, Guleria Kothi and Taj Ganges Avantika, the practical arrival/departure point is the hotel jetty at Namo Ghat, with the hotel providing the onward boat transfer complimentary as part of the booking package.

On or close to the ghats

A riverfront hotel gives you atmosphere from the moment you step outside. It can be wonderful for travellers who want to feel close to old Varanasi. The trade-off is access: the central ghat area is not designed for direct car movement, so the hotel choice affects how luggage, arrival and daily sightseeing are organised.

How BrijRama Palace, Guleria Kothi and Taj Ganges Avantika work

For these three hotels, our road vehicle takes guests to Namo Ghat. The hotels have their jetty there and provide complimentary arrival and departure boat transfers between Namo Ghat and the hotel. This is already part of the hotel booking package. We therefore coordinate the vehicle and luggage handover with the hotel rather than adding a separate paid boat transfer.

Other riverfront hotels may work differently

Do not assume every ghat hotel follows the same access system. Some may involve a walk through lanes, local transport or another hotel-managed arrangement. We confirm the exact procedure for the property and date rather than using a generic rule for all riverfront hotels.

Assi

Assi is popular with travellers who want the riverfront but prefer a slightly more open neighbourhood than the densest part of the old city. It works well for longer stays, cafés, morning walks and access to the southern ghats.

Nadesar and Cantonment

This side of the city is usually easier for vehicles and is home to several larger, more conventional hotels. It can suit mature travellers who value a quieter room, straightforward car access and a comfortable base between outings. You will travel to the ghats rather than live directly among them.

What luxury really means in Varanasi

A beautiful riverfront heritage hotel and a polished five-star city hotel offer two very different kinds of luxury. One gives you immersion and atmosphere. The other gives you space, easier access and hotel facilities. Neither is automatically better.

Hotel access is a sightseeing decision

Where you stay affects how every day begins and ends. A riverfront hotel may make sunrise much easier but requires more thought when connecting to a vehicle for Sarnath, BHU or airport transfers. A Cantonment hotel gives the car easy access but adds driving before sunrise. We therefore choose the hotel and itinerary together.

Ask about steps, lifts and the final approach

A hotel can advertise a lift and still be difficult for a guest with mobility concerns if the route outside involves steps or uneven lanes. Before recommending a riverfront property to senior travellers, we look at the practical final access rather than only the room category. The same applies to families with pushchairs and guests carrying large camera equipment.

River view is not the same as river access

Some hotels have a view towards the Ganges but are not directly on a usable ghat, while others open almost straight onto the riverfront. Ask what you actually want: to wake up overlooking the water, to begin a sunrise programme easily, or simply to stay in the old-city atmosphere.

Think about noise and sleep

The ghats and old city are living neighbourhoods, not resort zones. Bells, processions, loudspeakers and early religious activity are part of the atmosphere. Some guests find that magical; others sleep much better in a quieter city hotel and visit the old town with a guide.

Our usual recommendation process

We first ask about budget, mobility, preferred room style and how important the river is to you. Then we shortlist the areas that actually suit your trip. Once the property is selected, we adjust pickup times, boat arrangements and sightseeing sequence around its exact access.
