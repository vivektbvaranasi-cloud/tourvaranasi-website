Varanasi in January 2027: Weather, Fog, Ganga Aarti & 3-Day Itinerary

Plan Varanasi in January 2027: cold weather, fog, sunrise boat permissions, Ganga Aarti, Kashi Vishwanath, Sarnath and a relaxed private 3-day itinerary.

Varanasi in January at a glance

Varanasi’s January averages are 9.1°C overnight and 22.8°C by day, according to the India Meteorological Department’s 1981–2010 climate table. Check the daily forecast before your trip, especially if you plan to be on the river at dawn.

What to wear for a January sunrise boat ride

Wear a warm jacket over a removable layer, a scarf that covers your ears, and closed shoes with grip on the ghat steps. The wind feels colder when you are sitting still on the water. Keep the jacket with you for evening Aarti, even if the afternoon is warm enough for shirtsleeves.

A three-day January plan

  1. Arrival day: Walk the Northern Bazaar and watch Ganga Aarti from the ghat.
  2. First full morning: Plan a boat ride if visibility and river permissions allow, followed by Kashi Vishwanath. A lane walk works if boats start late. Leave the afternoon for a silk-weaving visit.
  3. Before departure: Visit Sarnath if your flight or train leaves enough time for the return drive. With an early departure, put Sarnath on the previous afternoon.

Can you take a sunrise boat ride in January?

A sunrise boat ride in Varanasi can be extraordinary when river operations are permitted and visibility is clear. The façades of the historic ghats gradually emerge in soft light, and the city begins its morning prayers along the water. But winter fog may make a very early departure impractical or unsafe.

Boat movements may be suspended or restricted by the authorities for safety, river conditions or other operational reasons. A private boat booking is never a guarantee that sailing will be permitted on the day. We confirm the applicable local rules close to travel and discuss a realistic replacement if necessary.

Our practical alternative when the river is unavailable

Instead of abandoning the morning, a local guide can arrange a ghat-side sunrise walk at a suitable hour. The riverfront, small shrines, traditional ceremonies and adjoining lanes are fascinating from land as well. A private walk may be especially rewarding when visibility is limited and the sound of the city matters more than the river panorama.

Where should you watch the evening Ganga Aarti in winter?

The evening Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat is a highlight of most first visits. In January, plan to arrive early enough to reach your viewing position without rushing through the congested approach lanes. Wear a warm layer for the ceremony.

We generally favour a thoughtfully chosen ghat or balcony viewing position over treating an offshore boat as the default place to watch. Depending on the availability and the family's preference, we can discuss reserved chairs or a Sankalp Aarti arrangement. These are different experiences; the most expensive option is not automatically the best for every traveller.

Visiting Kashi Vishwanath Temple in January

The cooler weather makes a visit to Shri Kashi Vishwanath Temple more comfortable than a hot summer afternoon, but it does not remove the walking involved. Private cars must stop outside the pedestrian-restricted inner-city approach. Temple queues, security screening, lockers and the day's official access procedures still apply.

Share any mobility needs before arriving. A practical guide helps plan the route and meeting point, but cannot promise a particular waiting time, special access or a temple-issued pass. Read our Kashi Vishwanath planning guide for the details to discuss beforehand.

A comfortable 3-day Varanasi itinerary for January 2027

For travellers who have not visited Kashi before, three days in Varanasi offer enough time to divide the riverfront, temples, culture and Buddhist heritage into manageable outings. The following is a framework rather than a rigid hour-by-hour promise.

Day 1 — Arrival, Northern Bazaar and Ganga Aarti

Meet your chauffeur at Varanasi Airport or railway station and transfer to your hotel. After check-in and time to rest, join your English-speaking local guide for a walk through the Northern Bazaar and selected old-city lanes. This introduction is more enjoyable when it is not rushed after a flight or train journey.

Continue to the riverfront for the evening Ganga Aarti, with a seating arrangement chosen in advance where available. Return to the hotel after the ceremony. Overnight in Varanasi.

Day 2 — Ganges morning, Kashi Vishwanath and silk weaving

Begin with a private river excursion if permitted and visibility allows, or enjoy the local guide's alternative ghat walk. Continue through the old city towards the Kashi Vishwanath Temple approach, allowing time for the necessary checks and queues. Return to the hotel for breakfast or rest, according to the final sequence.

In the afternoon, select a few city landmarks rather than trying to visit everything. Banaras Hindu University and the New Vishwanath Temple can be paired with a working Banarasi weaving experience that explains the craft without making shopping compulsory. Overnight in Varanasi.

Day 3 — Sarnath and onward departure

After breakfast, drive to Sarnath, the place traditionally associated with the Buddha's first sermon. Explore the Dhamek Stupa area, archaeological remains and Mulagandha Kuti Vihara with a guide. Include the archaeological museum when open; it is normally closed on Fridays.

Return to the city for your airport or station departure. Leave enough time for Varanasi traffic, particularly if departing after a holiday or busy weekend.
