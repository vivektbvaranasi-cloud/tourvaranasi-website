Staying on the Ghats: What Riverfront Hotels Change

A riverfront heritage hotel gives you direct access to the atmosphere of old Varanasi, but the arrival and sightseeing logistics are very different from a hotel that a car can reach directly.

Quick answer

For guests staying at BrijRama Palace, Guleria Kothi or Taj Ganges Avantika, the hotel jetty is at Namo Ghat. These hotels provide their own complimentary arrival and departure boat transfers from Namo Ghat as part of the hotel booking package. Your road vehicle therefore brings you to Namo Ghat; the hotel takes over the final river transfer.

Why this matters before you book

Good Varanasi planning is often about access and timing as much as sightseeing. A riverfront hotel can be one of the most memorable ways to stay in the city, but it does not operate like a conventional city hotel with door-to-door vehicle access. The transfer method should be understood before arrival so that guests know exactly where the driver finishes the road journey and where the hotel team takes over.

BrijRama Palace, Guleria Kothi and Taj Ganges Avantika

These three properties have their hotel jetty at Namo Ghat. On arrival, our vehicle brings guests and luggage to Namo Ghat, where the hotel coordinates the onward boat transfer to the property. The same arrangement works in reverse on departure. The boat transfer is complimentary and included in the hotel booking package, so it should not be presented as an extra Tour Varanasi charge or an optional paid transfer.

What staying on the riverfront really changes

A hotel on the ghats changes the feel of the entire trip. You can step out early and immediately be part of the riverfront instead of beginning every outing with a drive from Cantonment or Nadesar. The trade-off is that old Varanasi is not designed for direct vehicle access. For properties other than the three named above, the exact access may involve walking, local transport or another arrangement, so we confirm it property by property rather than assuming every ghat hotel works the same way.

Arrival and departure are hotel-managed at the jetty

For BrijRama Palace, Guleria Kothi and Taj Ganges Avantika, our driver and representative coordinate the road transfer up to Namo Ghat and the hotel staff manage the complimentary river transfer from their jetty. We plan enough time for luggage handover and boat boarding, especially for early airport or railway departures. River and administrative restrictions can occasionally affect operations, in which case the hotel advises the alternate access arrangement.

Who usually loves this style of hotel

Riverfront heritage hotels suit travellers who value atmosphere, architecture and the feeling of living inside old Varanasi. They are especially rewarding for guests who want to photograph the ghats at different times of day, enjoy walking, or prefer to return to the river between sightseeing. A guest who wants a large modern room, a simple car pickup from the lobby and minimal walking may be happier in a high-quality hotel around Nadesar or Cantonment.

Think about mobility before you book

The romantic description of a ghat hotel often leaves out steps. Riverfront movement can involve uneven stone, narrow passages and busy pedestrian areas. A lift inside the hotel does not remove every walking section outside it. For older travellers or anyone with knee, back or balance concerns, we ask a few practical questions before recommending the property and adjust the sightseeing pace accordingly.

How sightseeing changes from a ghat-side base

If you are already on the river, the sunrise programme can begin with less road travel, but Sarnath, BHU and other outlying sights still require a vehicle connection. We therefore avoid repeated back-and-forth journeys. A good day may begin on foot or by boat, continue to an agreed vehicle meeting point, cover the outer sights, then return to the riverfront later.

Our booking advice

Choose a riverfront heritage hotel because the location itself is part of your Varanasi experience. Tell us whether you prioritise river views, easy walking access, quiet sleep, large rooms, a pool, a lift or effortless vehicle pickup. We can then recommend the right style of hotel and build the sightseeing around its real access conditions.
