What does a private Ayodhya tour cost if you want to visit Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir, Hanuman Garhi, Kanak Bhawan and the Saryu riverfront without organising every transfer yourself? The answer depends mainly on how many days you have, how many people are travelling and whether you are beginning in Ayodhya or arriving from another city.

Our published Ayodhya tour prices start from Rs. 6,500 per person for one day, Rs. 8,500 for two days and Rs. 10,500 for three days. Each rate includes GST and is based on at least two guests. These are private ground-tour starting prices, not hotel-inclusive holiday packages.

What is included in our Ayodhya tour price?

  • Private local transport for the confirmed itinerary and agreed sightseeing duties.
  • An experienced local guide to explain the temples and help coordinate the day's sequence.
  • Listed local transfers, entrance and darshan-related arrangements included in the chosen tour, where permissible and specifically confirmed.
  • Local operating support to plan movement around pedestrian-only areas and temple security.
  • Applicable GST in the published starting rate.

What is excluded or needs separate confirmation?

  • Hotel accommodation, meals, refreshments and personal purchases.
  • Flights, train tickets and intercity transfers from Lucknow, Varanasi or elsewhere unless added to your quotation.
  • Personal pujas, donations, special rituals and any optional upgrade not listed in the confirmed itinerary.
  • Extra guide hours, out-of-town visits and additional private transport outside the agreed programme.
  • Tips, additional porterage and other personal costs.
Important: Ram Mandir entry and any special access follow the temple administration's current rules. Please do not treat a private tour quotation as a guaranteed VIP queue bypass or a confirmed Mangla Aarti ticket. We discuss only the assistance and arrangements actually available for your date.

How we would plan a one-day Ayodhya visit

With just one day, begin with the temple that matters most to you. We generally protect a substantial block of time for Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir because security, approach routes and crowds can change without much warning. Hanuman Garhi and Kanak Bhawan can be fitted around that visit, with the Ram Ki Paidi or Saryu riverfront added if time and energy permit.

It is tempting to add every temple seen on a map, but old Ayodhya works differently from an ordinary city tour. Private vehicles cannot reach every entrance. You may need an e-rickshaw followed by walking, and the final approach can be around 700–1,500 metres depending on restrictions. Tell us in advance if anyone is elderly or has difficulty walking.

Two days in Ayodhya: our preferred first-visit plan

Our one-night Ayodhya itinerary shows how to fit darshan around your arrival and departure. On the first afternoon, explore Ram Ki Paidi, Kale Ram Mandir and Nageshwarnath, then enjoy time by the Saryu. If river conditions and current regulations permit, an afternoon boat ride can be requested separately. We would plan this before sunset rather than assume evening boating will operate.

On the second day, make an early start for Ram Mandir darshan, followed by Hanuman Garhi and Kanak Bhawan. This order is adjusted to the confirmed access rules and the group's needs. A guide can make the visit easier to understand, but temple security and queue conditions remain outside any operator's control.

For the detailed programme, see our private two-day Ayodhya tour. Read the Saryu Aarti visitor guide before choosing front seating or Deep Daan. We confirm availability for your date.

Is a three-day Ayodhya tour worth the additional cost?

For senior travellers, families with children or guests who want time for prayer rather than constant sightseeing, the third day is valuable. It creates space to revisit a favourite temple or enjoy the riverfront more slowly. Optional visits towards Guptar Ghat, Nandigram or Chhapaiya can be considered after checking travel time and the final service scope.

Those outlying excursions are not automatically included simply because you book three days. We will identify any additional driving, guide services or entrance arrangements before confirming the price. See the three-day tour programme for the core option.

How much does Ayodhya cost from Lucknow or Varanasi?

These published Ayodhya rates assume the local programme described on the tour pages. Starting in Lucknow or Varanasi adds an intercity journey and may change the vehicle duty, tolls and number of practical sightseeing hours. A same-day Lucknow–Ayodhya–Lucknow private tour is possible for some groups, but its quotation is not the same as the Ayodhya local one-day starting price.

Send us your flight or train timings, hotel location, passenger count and luggage details. We can quote the correct route instead of giving you a misleading per-person amount. Travellers combining both sacred cities can also consider our Varanasi and Ayodhya private journey.

Frequently asked questions about Ayodhya tour pricing

Are hotels included in the Ayodhya packages?

No. The published rates above are for the described ground services without accommodation. We can recommend hotels near the temple and quote rooms separately according to date and category.

Can a solo traveller book a private Ayodhya tour?

Yes. However, one guest does not pay the two-guest per-person starting rate because the private vehicle and guide costs are shared by fewer people. Ask us for a solo quotation.

Does the package guarantee Ram Mandir VIP darshan?

No. All entry and special-access arrangements depend on the temple administration's procedures and availability. We will clearly identify what, if anything, can be assisted or prearranged.

Do senior citizens need to walk to Ram Mandir?

Usually there is still a last-mile approach despite private transport. Let us know about mobility restrictions or wheelchair needs before booking. See our Ayodhya wheelchair assistance guide for practical considerations.

Are the rates fixed during festivals?

No. These are indicative regular-date starting prices reviewed in November 2026. Festival surcharges, local movement restrictions and special arrangements require reconfirmation.
