Planning a private day trip from Varanasi to Prayagraj usually begins with one question: how much does it cost to visit Triveni Sangam with a car, local guide and boat? The trip is straightforward to arrange privately, but it involves a long road day, city access, the right riverboat arrangements and enough time at the confluence.

Our Varanasi to Prayagraj private day tour starts from Rs. 8,000 per person, including GST, based on two guests. For two adults, the starting package total is Rs. 16,000. The package includes a return private sedan, a local Prayagraj guide and a private boat at the Sangam, subject to river conditions and local permissions.

A realistic private Varanasi–Prayagraj itinerary

Early morning: leave Varanasi

We recommend an early hotel departure so that your day is not consumed by the road journey. Travel time depends on the pickup area, highway traffic, weather and city access. Your driver will take you to the agreed meeting point in Prayagraj, where the local guide joins the visit.

Late morning: private boat towards Triveni Sangam

Triveni Sangam, where the Ganga and Yamuna meet and the mythical Saraswati is traditionally revered, is the main reason for most pilgrimage day trips. When boats are authorised, board a private local boat for a closer approach to the confluence. Guests who wish to take a ritual dip should tell us beforehand, as changing arrangements, safety assistance and a priest-led ceremony are not automatic inclusions.

Boat services are controlled by the local administration and may change with water levels, wind, seasonal events and crowd restrictions. If boating is stopped, we will review what is possible on land and discuss the impact on the planned services; we cannot guarantee the river section against a safety closure.

Afternoon: selected temples or heritage sights

After the Sangam, choose one or two additional sites according to your interests and the available time. Bade Hanuman Ji Temple and Alopi Devi Temple suit a pilgrimage-focused day. Visitors interested in modern history can ask about Anand Bhavan, while Khusro Bagh is an alternative for Mughal-era architecture. We confirm opening days, ticket costs and the day's access before fixing the order.

Trying to include all these sites can make the return journey tiring. A private guide helps explain them, but a relaxed visit to Sangam plus two thoughtful stops is often better than racing through five attractions. If Anand Bhavan is essential, let us know before booking because museum opening arrangements may determine the route.

Late afternoon: return to Varanasi

Leave Prayagraj with enough daylight and road buffer for the return to your Varanasi hotel. We do not recommend combining this excursion with a fixed late-evening flight or train departure without discussing the connection risk. For mature travellers, a one-night stay in Prayagraj can make the experience much easier.

Private Sangam boat versus a shared local boat

Our starting tour price includes the private Sangam boat listed in the package, rather than requiring the guests to search for a boat on arrival. The exact boarding area, boat size and permissible route depend on the river's conditions and local instructions. When booking, tell us whether there are children, elderly guests or anyone who needs extra assistance getting into a boat.

During large pilgrimage events, including Magh Mela periods, boarding access and vehicle movement can be very different from an ordinary weekday. In those conditions, the regular-date package may need a revised quotation and an adjusted departure time. We will not promise a standard boat or car route if local authorities restrict it.

Should you stay overnight in Prayagraj?

The day tour is suitable for visitors based in Varanasi who mainly want Sangam and a small number of additional sites. It is less suitable for guests who want detailed museum visits, a slow ritual programme, or an unhurried day with limited driving. An overnight gives more time and reduces pressure to return the same evening.

Frequently asked questions about the day-trip cost

Is the Sangam boat included in Rs. 8,000?

Yes, one private Sangam boat is part of the published starting package, subject to the boat being permitted to operate on your date.

Are Prayagraj entrance tickets included?

No. Entrance fees for ticketed sights such as museums are additional, as are meals and any requested ritual services.

Can one person take this tour?

Yes. The published per-person price assumes at least two people sharing the private car and boat costs, so a solo guest receives a separate quotation.

Can the tour begin at Prayagraj and end in Varanasi?

We can customise the route, but a one-way service is a different quotation from the published same-day return tour.

Is a Sangam bath included?

The private boat arrangement is included. A ritual bath, priest, offerings, changing arrangements and other assistance must be requested and confirmed separately.
