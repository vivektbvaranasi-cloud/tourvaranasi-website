Varanasi to Prayagraj Private Day Tour

A private road excursion from Varanasi to Prayagraj focused on the Triveni Sangam and selected cultural sites, with realistic road and local access planning.

Why it matters

A simple, well-guided experience.

The Sangam is the reason to make this day meaningful, not a rushed list of city sights. Depending on operating conditions, take a local boat toward the confluence and add one or two sites such as Anand Bhavan, Bade Hanuman Ji or Khusro Bagh.

What you will experience

Your guide adapts the route to opening times, local conditions and the rest of your day. The experience is private or kept very small where possible.

Practical information

Wear comfortable shoes and modest clothing for religious areas. River activities remain subject to water levels and local rules.
