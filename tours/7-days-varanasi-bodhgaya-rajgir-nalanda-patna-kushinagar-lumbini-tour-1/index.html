7 Days Varanasi, Bodhgaya, Rajgir, Nalanda, Patna, Kushinagar & Lumbini Tour

A seven-day private Buddhist circuit linking Sarnath, Bodh Gaya, Rajgir, Nalanda, Kushinagar and Lumbini.

Journey introduction

A private journey with time to enjoy each place.

An ambitious private Buddhist circuit linking Sarnath, Bodh Gaya, Rajgir, Nalanda, Patna, Kushinagar and Lumbini. The itinerary is paced carefully because several days involve substantial road travel and an international border crossing.

We adjust the order around road or rail timing and the opening hours of Buddhist sites. Travel days are kept realistic rather than filled with extra sightseeing.

Itinerary

Your day-by-day itinerary

DAY 01

Arrive Varanasi — Old City & Ganga Aarti

After arrival, take time to settle before meeting your guide for a gentle introduction to old Varanasi. Walk through selected bazaar lanes and neighbourhoods towards the river, then watch the evening Ganga Aarti from a planned viewing position. The first day remains deliberately light so the journey begins comfortably after your flight or train.

Sightseeing: Northern Bazaar, old-city lanes, Dashashwamedh Ghat and evening Ganga Aarti

Overnight: Varanasi

DAY 02

Sunrise Varanasi & Sarnath

Begin on the Ganges before sunrise, followed by a short ghat walk and breakfast at the hotel. Later visit Sarnath, where the Buddha delivered his first sermon, and focus on the main archaeological and Buddhist sites. This provides useful historical context before the journey moves deeper into the Buddhist Circuit on the following days.

Sightseeing: Private sunrise boat, selected sacred Varanasi, Dhamek Stupa, Chaukhandi Stupa, archaeological ruins and Mulagandha Kuti Vihara

Overnight: Varanasi

DAY 03

Varanasi → Bodh Gaya

After breakfast, travel privately to Bodh Gaya with comfort stops along the way. Check into your hotel and use the late afternoon to rest after the road journey. If timing is favourable, make a first evening visit to the Mahabodhi Temple, when the illuminated complex and chanting offer a calm introduction before the detailed sightseeing planned for tomorrow.

Sightseeing: Private road transfer and optional evening Mahabodhi Temple visit

Overnight: Bodh Gaya

DAY 04

Bodh Gaya

Spend the day exploring the principal Buddhist sites at a measured pace. Begin with the Mahabodhi Temple and Bodhi Tree, then visit the Great Buddha Statue and selected international monasteries chosen around your interests. Sujata Village or Dungeshwari can be added for deeper historical context, provided the additional road time and walking suit your preferred pace.

Sightseeing: Mahabodhi Temple, Bodhi Tree, Great Buddha Statue, selected international monasteries, optional Sujata Village or Dungeshwari

Overnight: Bodh Gaya

DAY 05

Rajgir & Nalanda → Patna

Travel to Rajgir for selected Buddhist sites set among the surrounding hills, then continue the short distance to Nalanda. Walk through the ruins of the ancient monastic university and visit the museum when open before driving onward to Patna. It is a full touring day, so the itinerary concentrates on the places that add most meaning to the circuit.

Sightseeing: Rajgir, Vishwa Shanti Stupa / Vulture’s Peak as appropriate, Nalanda archaeological ruins and Nalanda Museum when open

Overnight: Patna

DAY 06

Patna → Kushinagar

Begin the substantial road journey to Kushinagar after breakfast, using suitable comfort stops on the way. A short Patna visit can be included first only when timings are sensible. On arrival, settle into the hotel and, if daylight remains, take an initial look at the Mahaparinirvana area rather than trying to complete the full Kushinagar programme late in the day.

Sightseeing: Optional Patna heritage stop and evening Mahaparinirvana area visit if timing permits

Overnight: Kushinagar

DAY 07

Kushinagar → Lumbini

Visit the principal Kushinagar Buddhist sites in the morning before continuing by road towards Lumbini in Nepal. Complete the India–Nepal border formalities and proceed to the birthplace of the Buddha, with travel time kept flexible because border processing can vary. Depending on arrival, begin with the Sacred Garden or keep the main Lumbini exploration for your onward programme.

Sightseeing: Mahaparinirvana Temple, Ramabhar Stupa, India–Nepal border crossing and Lumbini Sacred Garden when arrival time permits

Overnight: Lumbini / onward journey

Practical notes

Good to know

Practical questions

Questions travellers often ask

Is this a fast Buddhist Circuit?

It is a focused seven-day route and therefore involves regular travel. Guests who want more meditation time or a gentler pace should consider adding nights. Seven days works when the priority is to connect the principal pilgrimage sites, but it is not the right choice for someone wanting long meditation sessions or very slow mornings. There are several road sectors, so we keep sightseeing selective and avoid adding minor stops merely to fill the programme.

How much walking is required?

Moderate walking is expected at several archaeological and pilgrimage sites, with some uneven surfaces. In practice, we judge this more by the surfaces, crowds and access points than by kilometres alone, because those are what usually make a sightseeing day tiring. If you let us know your comfort level before arrival, we can shorten walking sections, add breaks and choose more convenient drop-off or meeting points wherever local rules allow.

Are local guides available?

Yes. Local English-speaking guides can be arranged at key destinations, while an accompanying escort can be considered for special-interest groups. For us, the value of a guide is not only in dates and history but in handling the practical side of a visit—where to enter, what is open, how much time to allow and when a route should be changed. We prefer guides who can read the guest’s interest and pace instead of delivering the same memorised commentary to everyone.

What should we know about Lumbini?

Lumbini is in Nepal, so passport, visa and border requirements depend on nationality and current rules. These should be checked for the actual travel dates. Because the journey crosses an international border, we check passport validity, visa rules and the practical border procedure for the traveller’s nationality before finalising the route. Border timings and documentation requirements can change, so this is something we reconfirm for the actual travel dates rather than relying on an old itinerary.

Can the route be customised?

Yes. We can add or remove stops, allow more time in Rajgir or Bodh Gaya, and change transport according to the guest profile. Private touring gives us room to make that adjustment properly, so a day does not have to be completed exactly as it appears on the website. We usually decide which experiences are essential for you, then remove or move the less important stops rather than compressing everything into a tiring schedule.
