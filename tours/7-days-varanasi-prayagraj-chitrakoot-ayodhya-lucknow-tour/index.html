Itinerary

Your day-by-day itinerary

DAY 01

Arrive Varanasi — Old City & Ganga Aarti

After arrival and time to settle, meet your guide for a gentle first experience of Banaras. Walk through selected bazaar lanes and old neighbourhoods towards the river, with time to understand the city rather than simply passing through it. Finish at the evening Ganga Aarti from a suitable viewing point and keep the remainder of the evening free.

Sightseeing: Northern Bazaar, old-city lanes, Dashashwamedh Ghat and evening Ganga Aarti

Overnight: Varanasi

DAY 02

Sunrise Varanasi & Sarnath

Begin before sunrise with a private boat along the ghats, followed by a short walk and breakfast at the hotel. Later visit selected sacred and cultural Varanasi before travelling to Sarnath for its Buddhist archaeological remains and temples. The day is paced so you can return to the hotel between the early start and afternoon sightseeing if you prefer.

Sightseeing: Private sunrise boat, selected sacred Varanasi, Dhamek Stupa, Chaukhandi Stupa and Mulagandha Kuti Vihara

Overnight: Varanasi

DAY 03

Varanasi → Prayagraj

Drive privately to Prayagraj and begin with the Triveni Sangam, reached by local boat when operating. Continue with a selection of the city’s religious and historical sites rather than trying to cover every landmark. Your guide will adjust the order around traffic and opening times, while the overnight stay keeps the day comfortable before Chitrakoot.

Sightseeing: Triveni Sangam, Bade Hanuman Ji, Anand Bhavan, Alopi Devi and/or Khusro Bagh

Overnight: Prayagraj

DAY 04

Chitrakoot Day Excursion

Travel to Chitrakoot for a day centred on the Ramayana landscape and the Mandakini riverfront. Spend time at Ram Ghat before continuing to a small selection of sacred sites chosen around road time, walking and local conditions. The emphasis is on understanding the destination without creating an exhausting checklist, then return to Prayagraj for the night.

Sightseeing: Ram Ghat, Mandakini riverfront and selected Chitrakoot sites such as Kamadgiri or Sati Anusuya where practical

Overnight: Prayagraj

DAY 05

Prayagraj → Ayodhya

Leave Prayagraj for Ayodhya and check into your hotel on arrival. If timing allows, begin with Nageshwarnath Temple, Ram Ki Paidi and the Saryu riverfront rather than entering the busiest temple area late in the day. Attend the Saryu Aarti in the evening, with local transport arranged where vehicle restrictions require it.

Sightseeing: Nageshwarnath Temple, Ram Ki Paidi, Saryu riverfront and evening Saryu Aarti

Overnight: Ayodhya

DAY 06

Ayodhya → Lucknow

Start early for the main sacred sites of Ayodhya. Your guide will coordinate walking, e-rickshaw movement and security around the temple precinct before you return to the vehicle and continue towards Lucknow. Arrive in the state capital later in the day and keep the evening at leisure, or add a gentle food experience if you would like one.

Sightseeing: Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir, Hanuman Garhi, Kanak Bhawan, Dashrath Mahal and onward drive to Lucknow

Overnight: Lucknow

DAY 07

Lucknow & Departure

Explore a compact selection of Lucknow’s Nawabi heritage before your onward departure. Visit the Bara Imambara and Rumi Darwaza, then continue to another major site such as the British Residency or Chhota Imambara according to the time available. The final sightseeing is planned around your flight or train rather than asking you to rush back to the airport.

Sightseeing: Bara Imambara, Rumi Darwaza, Chhota Imambara and/or British Residency according to departure time

Overnight: Departure from Lucknow
