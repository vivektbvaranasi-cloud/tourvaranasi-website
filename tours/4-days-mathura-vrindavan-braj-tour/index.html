3 nights / 4 days · Private Braj journey

Mathura, Vrindavan, Govardhan, Barsana & Nandgaon

Braj needs time. We keep a full day for Vrindavan and a separate day for Govardhan, Barsana and Nandgaon instead of compressing the region into a rushed single-day temple circuit.

Our planning rule

Minimum two full sightseeing days in Braj.

The map makes Mathura and Vrindavan look close, but a meaningful Braj visit is governed by temple timings, darshan queues, pedestrian lanes and the fact that different sacred sites come alive at different times of day.

If a journey includes only the core Mathura and Vrindavan temples, we still recommend at least two days. When the guest also wants Govardhan, Barsana and Nandgaon, our preferred product is 3 nights / 4 days: Mathura on arrival, one full Vrindavan day, one full wider-Braj day and departure on Day 4.

Itinerary

Your day-by-day Braj itinerary

DAY 01

Delhi / Agra — Mathura — Vrindavan

Arrive in the Braj region and begin with Mathura rather than trying to squeeze all of Mathura and Vrindavan into the same sightseeing block. Visit Shri Krishna Janmabhoomi and, subject to arrival time and local conditions, Vishram Ghat or a selected Yamuna riverfront area. Continue to Vrindavan, check in and rest. If timing works comfortably, visit Prem Mandir in the evening for the illuminated temple atmosphere; otherwise keep it for the following day.

Sightseeing: Shri Krishna Janmabhoomi, Vishram Ghat / Mathura riverfront and optional evening Prem Mandir

Overnight: Vrindavan

DAY 02

Full Day Vrindavan — Temples, Heritage Lanes & Evening Atmosphere

Keep the entire day for Vrindavan. Visit Banke Bihari Temple, ISKCON Krishna Balaram Mandir, Radha Raman Temple, Nidhivan and Seva Kunj, allowing for temple opening breaks and changing darshan queues. The main vehicle cannot approach every old-city temple, so use local e-rickshaw / battery mobility where practical and expect some walking through narrow lanes. In the evening return towards Prem Mandir, Keshi Ghat or another appropriate river / temple area according to timing and the guest's interest.

Sightseeing: Banke Bihari, ISKCON Krishna Balaram Mandir, Radha Raman, Nidhivan, Seva Kunj, heritage lanes, Prem Mandir and Keshi Ghat as timing permits

Overnight: Vrindavan

DAY 03

Govardhan — Barsana — Nandgaon

After breakfast drive to Govardhan and visit Mansi Ganga, Kusum Sarovar and Radha Kund along with selected sacred points associated with Govardhan. A full 21-km Govardhan Parikrama is not automatically added to this day because it materially changes the timing and should be planned separately if requested. Continue to Barsana for Shri Radha Rani Temple, using local-access assistance where the main vehicle cannot approach. Thereafter continue to Nandgaon and Nand Bhavan before returning to Vrindavan in the evening.

Sightseeing: Mansi Ganga, Kusum Sarovar, Radha Kund, selected Govardhan sacred points, Shri Radha Rani Temple Barsana, Nandgaon and Nand Bhavan

Overnight: Vrindavan

DAY 04

Vrindavan — Departure

After breakfast check out and proceed for Delhi, Agra, Mathura railway station or your onward destination. Keep a realistic road buffer when travelling to Delhi Airport because NCR traffic can affect the final approach. We do not add a long list of temples to departure morning unless the actual departure timing makes it comfortable.

Sightseeing: Departure only; optional short visit only when timings genuinely allow

Departure: According to onward journey

Why not one day?

Temple hours break the day into windows.

Several Braj temples close during the middle of the day, while Banke Bihari and other popular shrines can have heavy queues and changing crowd-control routes. A single compressed list may look efficient on paper but often results in guests seeing gates, rushing meals or spending most of the day in local traffic.

Local mobility

Large vehicles do not reach every temple.

Old Vrindavan and some Barsana approaches require e-rickshaw, battery vehicle or walking from the practical vehicle point. We factor this into the itinerary. It is particularly important for senior travellers, because the distance shown on a map does not reflect the actual effort of navigating temple approaches.

Practical questions

What travellers should know before Braj

Can we do Mathura and Vrindavan in one day?

You can visit a small selection of major temples, but we do not describe that as a proper Braj itinerary. Our website itineraries now use two full sightseeing days as the minimum practical standard, and the wider Govardhan-Barsana-Nandgaon circuit receives its own day.

Is VIP darshan guaranteed?

No. Where an official priority facility exists we can arrange or assist with it, but old Braj temples have their own operating traditions and crowd controls. Banke Bihari in particular should never be sold with a guaranteed out-of-turn darshan promise.

Can we add the full Govardhan Parikrama?

Yes, but it should be planned as a specific requirement. A full 21-km parikrama changes the day's pace and local transport arrangements, so we do not quietly insert it into the standard Govardhan-Barsana-Nandgaon excursion.

Where should we stay?

For this programme we prefer one base in Vrindavan for all three nights. It avoids unnecessary packing and lets Day 2 begin directly with Vrindavan's temple timings while Day 3 runs as a straightforward excursion to the wider Braj sites.
