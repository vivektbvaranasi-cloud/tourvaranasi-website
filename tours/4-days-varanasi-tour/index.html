4 Days Varanasi Tour

Four days lets you see Varanasi at an easier pace, with time for Sarnath, craft, rest between outings and an unhurried departure.

Journey introduction

A comfortable way to see Varanasi.

This is our gentlest Varanasi-only programme, designed for travellers who want time to observe the city rather than rush through a checklist.

Evening Aarti is best planned from an elevated balcony for a clear overall view or through Sankalp participation for guests who want a closer spiritual experience. The sunrise boat and old-city sequence are kept together so the programme avoids unnecessary backtracking.

Itinerary

Your day-by-day itinerary

DAY 01

Arrival — Old City & Evening Ganga Aarti

Arrive at Varanasi Airport or Varanasi Railway Station and transfer privately to your hotel. Keep the first day intentionally light. Later meet your guide and walk through the Northern Bazaar and selected old-city lanes, gradually reaching the river. For the evening Ganga Aarti, choose an elevated balcony for the clearest overall view or Sankalp Aarti participation for a closer spiritual experience. Reserved chairs can be arranged for comfort. Boat viewing is used mainly when a guest specifically prefers the river perspective.

Sightseeing: Northern Bazaar, old-city lanes, Dashashwamedh Ghat and evening Ganga Aarti by balcony, Sankalp participation or reserved seating

Overnight: Varanasi

DAY 02

Sunrise Boat — Manikarnika — Chowk Markets — Kashi Vishwanath

Start before sunrise with a private motor-boat ride of around one hour. Continue towards Manikarnika Ghat, where the guide can sensitively explain the traditions surrounding cremation and moksha; this part is omitted when a guest does not wish to see the cremation area. Continue on foot through the Chowk flower market, traditional perfume market and Kachori Gali to the Kashi Vishwanath Temple area. Optional assisted priority / Sugam-style darshan is arranged only where the official facility is operating. Afterwards walk to the agreed vehicle point and return to the hotel for breakfast and rest.

Later visit selected sacred and cultural sites including Sankat Mochan, Tulsi Manas Temple, BHU & New Vishwanath Temple and Bharat Mata Temple at an unhurried pace.

Sightseeing: Private sunrise motor boat, optional Manikarnika Ghat, Chowk flower market, perfume market, Kachori Gali, Kashi Vishwanath, Sankat Mochan, Tulsi Manas, BHU & New Vishwanath Temple and Bharat Mata Temple

Overnight: Varanasi

DAY 03

Sarnath & Banarasi Craft

Travel to Sarnath and give the site the time it deserves. Visit Dhamek Stupa, Chaukhandi Stupa, the archaeological ruins and Mulagandha Kuti Vihara, including the Archaeological Museum when open. After returning to Varanasi, spend time with the city's craft tradition through a Banarasi weaving or textile experience, focusing on the handloom process and the skill behind the finished fabric rather than treating it as a shopping stop.

Sightseeing: Sarnath archaeological and Buddhist sites plus Banarasi weaving / textile experience

Overnight: Varanasi

DAY 04

Varanasi — Departure

After breakfast, check out from the hotel. According to your confirmed flight or train timing, proceed by private vehicle to Varanasi Airport or Varanasi Railway Station for your onward journey. If the departure is later in the day, a short food, craft, photography or riverfront experience can be added only when there is comfortable time; the departure transfer remains the priority.

Programme: Breakfast, hotel check-out and private departure transfer; optional short experience only when timing genuinely permits

Departure: Private transfer to Varanasi Airport / Railway Station

Riverfront hotel access

Namo Ghat hotel jetty

Guests staying at BrijRama Palace, Guleria Kothi or Taj Ganges Avantika use the hotels' jetty at Namo Ghat. Complimentary hotel-operated arrival and departure boat transfers from Namo Ghat are included in the hotel booking package.

Practical notes

Flexible without being vague

River conditions, temple procedures, traffic controls and departure timings can change the exact order. We keep enough structure for the trip to run smoothly while protecting rest time and the final airport or railway transfer.

The final day is not filled with compulsory sightseeing. Departure comes first.
Practical questions

Good to know

Who is the 4-day Varanasi tour best for?

It suits travellers who want the main experiences without filling every hour and mature travellers who value more rest between outings.

Is the sunrise boat compulsory?

No. It is recommended but remains subject to river operation and can be replaced with a riverfront experience when boats are suspended.

Can the programme be slowed down?

Yes. The four-day structure is deliberately flexible, so walking routes can be shortened and sightseeing divided into smaller blocks.

What happens on Day 4?

Day 4 is the departure day. After breakfast and check-out, we provide a private transfer to Varanasi Airport or Varanasi Railway Station. An optional short visit is considered only when the confirmed onward timing allows it comfortably.
