Start with the river, old city and sacred landmarks, then continue to Ayodhya, Prayagraj or the Buddhist heartlands if you have more time. Every journey is privately arranged and planned around how these places actually work on the ground.

A practical place to begin

How many days do you actually need?

There is no single “right” Varanasi itinerary. What matters is how much you want to see, how early you are comfortable starting, and whether you prefer a full day or some breathing room between experiences.

1 day

Possible, but expect a full day with an early start. Best when time is genuinely limited.

2 days

Our recommendation for a first visit. Enough time for the river, old city, temples and Sarnath.

3 days

Better if you want a walk, craft or food experience without rushing between places.

For travellers who prefer slower mornings, deeper neighbourhood time and more flexibility.
