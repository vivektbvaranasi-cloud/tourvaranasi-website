Guided Varanasi tours

Private travel built around how Varanasi actually works.

The river, old city, temple access and road movement need to be connected intelligently. That is where a local private guide and flexible plan matter most.

1 Day Varanasi Tour

A focused private day for travellers with one full day in Varanasi, combining the river at dawn, an old-city introduction, Sarnath and the evening Ganga Aarti without treating the city as a checklist.

2 days · Private

2 Days Varanasi Tour

Our flagship first visit to Varanasi: an old-city introduction and evening Aarti on the first day, followed by sunrise on the Ganges, sacred and cultural Varanasi, and Sarnath on the second.

3 Days Varanasi Tour

Three days gives Varanasi more breathing room: river and old city, sacred and Buddhist heritage, then a deeper layer chosen around craft, food, neighbourhoods or the southern ghats.

4 Days Varanasi Tour

Varanasi at a slower, deeper pace — designed for travellers who prefer time to observe, recover between outings and add one or two meaningful cultural experiences rather than rushing through more sights.

5 Days Varanasi & Bodhgaya Tour

6 Days Varanasi, Bodhgaya & Prayagraj Tour

A private journey joining Varanasi and Sarnath with Bodh Gaya’s Buddhist heritage and the sacred confluence at Prayagraj.
