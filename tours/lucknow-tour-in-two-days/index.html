Lucknow Tour in Two Days

Discover Lucknow with a private guide: Nawabi monuments, old-city streets, the Residency and time for the food and craft traditions of Awadh. A two-day stay balances the essential sights with time to rest.

Your private itinerary

2 days in Lucknow

This suggested programme can be adjusted around your arrival, departure and interests. This programme assumes you are ready to begin sightseeing on the first morning. If you arrive later, we will adjust the visits. Hotels can be arranged or you can use your own bookings.

Your day-by-day programme

Day 1

Bara Imambara, Rumi Darwaza & old Lucknow

Meet your guide at your Lucknow hotel and explore Bara Imambara, including the Bhool Bhulaiya when access and mobility permit. Continue to Rumi Darwaza for a photo stop and visit Chota Imambara. After a lunch break, take a guided walk through selected Chowk lanes to discover the old city’s shops, architecture and everyday life. Return to the hotel with time to relax.

Overnight: Lucknow

Day 2

The Residency, city heritage & Hazratganj & departure

Spend the morning at the Residency, exploring the historic grounds with your guide. Continue to La Martiniere for an exterior view; entry is included only if permission has been arranged. After lunch, visit Ambedkar Memorial and enjoy a relaxed stroll through Hazratganj, with time to browse independently. Later, transfer to the airport or railway station. An evening departure allows the full programme; we will shorten the final day if you need to leave earlier.

Departure: Lucknow airport or railway station.

Monument access and the order of visits are checked for your travel dates. The Bhool Bhulaiya involves steps and narrow passages. La Martiniere interior access, artisan meetings and any hosted dining experience require advance confirmation; none is guaranteed by this sample itinerary.

What we can arrange

Private air-conditioned transport, a local English-speaking guide, airport or railway transfers and the sightseeing listed above can be combined in your quotation. We confirm every inclusion before booking.

Hotel accommodation, entrance tickets, meals, local e-rickshaws, special darshan arrangements and optional experiences are quoted separately unless expressly included in your proposal. Personal expenses and tips are additional.
