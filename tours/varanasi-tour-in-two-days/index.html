2 Days Varanasi Tour

Old-city life and a carefully chosen Ganga Aarti experience on Day 1, followed by sunrise on the Ganges, the living lanes of Chowk, Kashi Vishwanath and Sarnath on Day 2.

Journey introduction

A first visit planned around how Varanasi actually works.

Two days can work very well when the programme follows the natural rhythm of the city rather than repeatedly moving between the hotel, ghats and old city.

The evening Aarti is arranged from a balcony when available for the best overall view, or through Sankalp participation for guests who want a closer spiritual experience. The following morning, we combine the sunrise boat with the old-city walk and temple approach in one continuous sequence.

Itinerary

Your day-by-day itinerary

DAY 01

Arrival — Old City Introduction & Evening Ganga Aarti

After arrival and time to settle at the hotel, meet your private guide for an easy introduction to the old city and riverfront. Walk through selected bazaar lanes before approaching Dashashwamedh Ghat for the evening ceremony. Our preferred arrangement is an elevated balcony viewpoint, which gives a clear view of the complete formation of priests, or Sankalp Aarti participation for guests who want to sit closer and take part in the ritual. Reserved chair seating is a useful comfort option. We do not automatically recommend boat viewing for Aarti because the boat can be farther from the priests and the view may be blocked by other boats.

Sightseeing: Northern Bazaar / old-city lanes, Dashashwamedh Ghat and evening Ganga Aarti by balcony, Sankalp participation or reserved seating according to preference and availability

Overnight: Varanasi

DAY 02

Sunrise Boat — Manikarnika — Chowk Markets — Kashi Vishwanath — Sarnath

Leave before sunrise for a private motor-boat ride of around one hour on the Ganges. Continue along the river towards Manikarnika Ghat, where your guide explains Varanasi's beliefs around death and liberation with sensitivity; this section is completely optional and is omitted for guests who do not wish to experience the cremation area. From here continue on foot through the living old city: the Chowk flower market, traditional perfume market and Kachori Gali, before reaching the Kashi Vishwanath Temple area. Optional assisted priority / Sugam-style darshan can be arranged where the official facility and temple procedures permit. Walk back to the agreed vehicle point and return to the hotel for breakfast and rest.

Later continue with selected cultural sights such as Sankat Mochan Temple, Tulsi Manas Temple, BHU & New Vishwanath Temple before driving to Sarnath. Visit Dhamek Stupa, Chaukhandi Stupa and Mulagandha Kuti Vihara; the Archaeological Museum is included when open and should not be planned on its weekly closure day. Return to Varanasi or continue for departure according to your schedule.

Sightseeing: Private sunrise motor boat, Manikarnika Ghat optional, Chowk flower market, perfume market, Kachori Gali, Kashi Vishwanath, Sankat Mochan, Tulsi Manas, BHU & New Vishwanath Temple, Dhamek Stupa, Chaukhandi Stupa and Mulagandha Kuti Vihara

Overnight: Varanasi / departure

Riverfront hotel access

BrijRama, Guleria Kothi & Taj Ganges Avantika

Guests staying at BrijRama Palace, Guleria Kothi or Taj Ganges Avantika use the hotels' jetty at Namo Ghat. The hotels provide complimentary boat transfers for arrival and departure from Namo Ghat as part of the hotel booking package. Our road vehicle therefore takes the guest to Namo Ghat and the hotel manages the final river transfer.

Practical note

Why the morning is kept continuous

The boat, riverfront, Chowk markets and Kashi Vishwanath all sit naturally in one sequence. Returning to the hotel immediately after the boat and driving back into the old city wastes time and repeats the difficult access section. We therefore finish the old-city walk first, meet the vehicle afterwards, and only then return for breakfast.

Cremation ghats are living ritual spaces. We explain them sensitively, discourage intrusive photography and omit the visit completely when it does not suit the guest.
Practical questions

Questions travellers often ask

Is two days enough for Varanasi?

Yes for a first introduction, particularly when Day 1 has enough usable time for the old city and evening Aarti. The programme above avoids unnecessary backtracking and still leaves breakfast/rest time after the early morning. Travellers who want food walks, silk weaving, music or a slower pace should consider three days.

Is balcony viewing better than a boat for Aarti?

For most guests, yes. A balcony gives a stable elevated view of the complete ceremony and keeps you closer to the sound and atmosphere. Boat viewing gives a beautiful river perspective, but several rows of boats can form and the view may be obstructed, so we recommend it only when that river perspective is specifically what the guest wants.

Can we skip Manikarnika Ghat?

Absolutely. It is included only for travellers who are comfortable learning about the cremation traditions and beliefs around liberation in Varanasi. If a guest prefers not to see the cremation area, the guide modifies the old-city route and continues towards the markets and Kashi Vishwanath without making it part of the experience.

Can the car take us directly to Kashi Vishwanath?

No. The final central section is pedestrian and the practical drop point changes with local traffic controls. Benia Parking is commonly used from this side, after which the guide walks with the guest through the old city and later coordinates the agreed vehicle meeting point.
