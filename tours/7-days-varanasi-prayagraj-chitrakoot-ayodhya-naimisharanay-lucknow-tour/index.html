Itinerary

Your day-by-day itinerary

DAY 01

Arrive Varanasi — Old City & Ganga Aarti

After arrival, take time to settle before meeting your guide for a relaxed introduction to old Varanasi. Walk through selected bazaar and neighbourhood lanes towards the river, stopping where useful to understand the traditions and daily life around you. End with the evening Ganga Aarti from a planned viewing position and return to the hotel without adding unnecessary sightseeing.

Sightseeing: Northern Bazaar, old-city lanes, Dashashwamedh Ghat and evening Ganga Aarti

Overnight: Varanasi

DAY 02

Sunrise Varanasi & Sarnath

Begin before sunrise on a private boat along the ghats, followed by a short walk and breakfast at the hotel. Continue later with selected sacred and cultural Varanasi before travelling to Sarnath. The day gives equal importance to the living riverfront and the Buddhist site where the Buddha gave his first sermon, without making either feel rushed.

Sightseeing: Private sunrise boat, selected sacred Varanasi, Dhamek Stupa, Chaukhandi Stupa and Mulagandha Kuti Vihara

Overnight: Varanasi

DAY 03

Varanasi → Prayagraj

Drive privately to Prayagraj and visit the Triveni Sangam by local boat when operating. Continue with a manageable selection of the city’s religious and historical sights, with the order adjusted around opening times and traffic. Overnight in Prayagraj so the day does not become a long transfer followed by an unnecessarily late road journey.

Sightseeing: Triveni Sangam, Bade Hanuman Ji, Anand Bhavan, Alopi Devi and/or Khusro Bagh

Overnight: Prayagraj

DAY 04

Chitrakoot Excursion

Travel to Chitrakoot for a private day excursion focused on the Mandakini riverfront and the destination’s Ramayana associations. Begin at Ram Ghat and continue to selected sacred sites based on your interest and comfort with walking. Return to Prayagraj in the evening, keeping the programme selective enough to allow a meaningful visit rather than a rushed pilgrimage circuit.

Sightseeing: Ram Ghat, Mandakini riverfront and selected Chitrakoot sacred sites such as Kamadgiri or Sati Anusuya where practical

Overnight: Prayagraj

DAY 05

Prayagraj → Ayodhya

After breakfast, drive to Ayodhya and check into your hotel. If arrival time permits, begin with the easier riverfront programme around Nageshwarnath Temple, Ram Ki Paidi and the Saryu rather than entering the busiest temple area immediately. Attend the Saryu Aarti in the evening, with local e-rickshaw or walking arrangements used where vehicles are restricted.

Sightseeing: Nageshwarnath Temple, Ram Ki Paidi, Saryu riverfront and evening Saryu Aarti

Overnight: Ayodhya

DAY 06

Ayodhya → Naimisharanya → Lucknow

Start with the principal sacred sites of Ayodhya before leaving the city, with your guide coordinating the local access and walking around the temple precinct. Continue by private vehicle to Naimisharanya, an important pilgrimage centre, and visit the main sacred points without overloading the stop. Drive onward to Lucknow for the night and a relaxed evening.

Sightseeing: Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir, Hanuman Garhi, Kanak Bhawan, Naimisharanya and selected local sacred sites

Overnight: Lucknow

DAY 07

Lucknow & Departure

Spend the final morning with a concise introduction to Lucknow’s Nawabi architecture and history. Visit the Bara Imambara and Rumi Darwaza, adding the British Residency or Chhota Imambara when your departure timing allows. Your private transfer then continues to Lucknow Airport or railway station with a comfortable margin before departure.

Sightseeing: Bara Imambara, Rumi Darwaza, Chhota Imambara and/or British Residency according to onward timing

Overnight: Departure from Lucknow
