Ayodhya Tour in Two Days

Visit Ram Mandir and the temples of Ayodhya with a local guide, time by the Saryu and a private itinerary adjusted to your pace. A two-day stay balances the essential sights with time to rest.

Your private itinerary

2 days in Ayodhya

This suggested programme can be adjusted around your arrival, departure and interests. Plan an arrival by early afternoon on the first day. Hotels can be arranged or you can use your own bookings.

Your day-by-day programme

Day 1

Arrival, Ram Ki Paidi & Saryu Aarti

Arrive at Ayodhya Airport or railway station and transfer to your hotel. After time to settle in, meet your local guide for an introduction to the sacred city. Visit Ram Ki Paidi and Nageshwarnath Temple as opening hours permit, then attend Saryu Aarti before returning to your hotel. Keep the afternoon flexible if your arrival is late.

Overnight: Ayodhya

Day 2

Ram Mandir & the temple quarter & departure

Plan the morning around Ram Janmabhoomi darshan, allowing time for security and the queue. Continue to Hanuman Garhi, Kanak Bhawan and Dashrath Mahal at a comfortable pace, with a break between visits. Local e-rickshaws and walking are used in restricted areas. Hanuman Garhi involves steps and can be omitted for guests who prefer an easier route. Later, transfer to the airport or railway station. An evening departure allows the full programme; we will shorten the final day if you need to leave earlier.

Departure: Ayodhya airport or railway station.

Temple entry follows the official rules on your travel date. We do not promise a fixed queue time or guaranteed priority access. Expect security checks, walking and local e-rickshaws near the temples; please tell us about mobility needs before booking.

What we can arrange

Private air-conditioned transport, a local English-speaking guide, airport or railway transfers and the sightseeing listed above can be combined in your quotation. We confirm every inclusion before booking.

Hotel accommodation, entrance tickets, meals, local e-rickshaws, special darshan arrangements and optional experiences are quoted separately unless expressly included in your proposal. Personal expenses and tips are additional.
