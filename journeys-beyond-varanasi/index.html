Journeys Beyond Varanasi

Link Varanasi with Ayodhya, Prayagraj, Bodh Gaya and the Buddhist heartlands through road journeys that keep travel time and local realities in view.

Extend the journey

Varanasi is a natural starting point for several very different cultural routes.

Choose a Ramayana-focused road journey through Prayagraj and Ayodhya, or follow the Buddhist geography from Sarnath to Bodh Gaya, Rajgir, Nalanda, Kushinagar and Lumbini.

5 Days Varanasi & Ayodhya Tour

A five-day private journey linking the layered river culture of Varanasi with Ayodhya’s Ramayana landscape, with a full day in each city rather than a rushed same-day transfer.

5 Days Varanasi & Bodhgaya Tour

A compact private journey connecting Varanasi and Sarnath with Bodh Gaya, where the Buddha attained enlightenment — ideal for travellers interested in both Hindu and Buddhist sacred geography.

6 Days Varanasi, Prayagraj & Ayodhya Tour

A six-day private journey through three important north Indian sacred landscapes: Varanasi’s riverfront, the Sangam at Prayagraj and Ayodhya’s Ramayana sites.

6 Days Varanasi, Bodhgaya & Prayagraj Tour

A private journey joining Varanasi and Sarnath with Bodh Gaya’s Buddhist heritage and the sacred confluence at Prayagraj.

6 Days Varanasi, Bodhgaya, Rajgir, Nalanda & Patna Tour

A concise Buddhist and cultural journey from Varanasi and Sarnath through Bodh Gaya to Rajgir, Nalanda and Patna, with the major sites arranged in a logical road sequence.

7 Days Varanasi, Prayagraj, Chitrakoot, Ayodhya & Lucknow Tour

A seven-day cultural road journey from Varanasi through Prayagraj and Chitrakoot to Ayodhya, finishing in Lucknow.
