Privacy

Privacy Policy

How we handle information submitted while planning or operating your journey.

Your information

Privacy, in plain language.

We collect only the information reasonably needed to answer your enquiry, plan your trip and operate services you ask us to arrange.

Information we may collect

When you contact Tour Varanasi through our website, email, telephone or WhatsApp, you may provide your name, email address, telephone number, nationality or country of residence, travel dates, number of travellers, hotel preferences, mobility or dietary requirements and other details relevant to your journey.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to enquiries, prepare itineraries and quotations, confirm travel arrangements, communicate operational information and provide support connected with a potential or confirmed booking.

Hotels, guides, drivers and other suppliers

For a confirmed journey, we may share the limited information required by a hotel, guide, driver, boat operator, ticketing provider or other service partner involved in delivering your arrangements. We do not sell your personal information to advertisers or unrelated marketing companies.

Website forms and technical providers

Our website hosting and form-processing providers may technically process enquiry information in order to transmit or store a form submission. They are used only as part of operating the website and handling your request.

Marketing

We will not add you to promotional communication merely because you requested a quotation. If we later offer an optional newsletter or marketing update, you can choose whether to subscribe and can opt out at any time.

Cookies and technical logs

The website does not currently rely on advertising cookies. Like most hosted websites, basic technical logs may be created for security, reliability and website operation.

Keeping information

Booking and correspondence records may be retained where reasonably needed for customer service, accounting, legal or operational purposes. Information that is no longer reasonably required may be deleted or anonymised.

Your questions

If you would like to ask what information we hold about a booking or request a correction, contact tours@tourvaranasi.com.
