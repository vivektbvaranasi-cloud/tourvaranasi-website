Banarasi Silk & Weaving Experience

See how Banarasi silk is made and meet people connected to the city’s handloom tradition.

Why it matters

A simple, well-guided experience.

A weaving visit is most worthwhile when it goes beyond shopping. The emphasis is on watching the loom, understanding how patterns are built and meeting people connected to the craft.

What you will experience

Workshop visits depend on the weaver or studio being available. The focus is on understanding the craft, with shopping only if you want it.

Around 2 hours

Approximate duration

We fit the timing around your hotel, arrival schedule and other sightseeing.

Private experience advantage

Your guide has time to explain, answer questions and adjust the pace around you.

Practical information

Some workshops are in residential neighbourhoods, so access may involve short walks and stairs.
