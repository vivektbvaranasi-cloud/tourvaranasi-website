Local information matters.

Temple procedures, river operations, traffic restrictions and festival movement can change. We prefer to confirm what is workable rather than promise what looks attractive on paper.

Practical planning

We prefer to tell you what will actually work.

A good itinerary should work on the ground. Traffic restrictions, temple procedures, river conditions, local events, drive times and crowd movement can all change how a day should be planned.

We therefore prefer realistic programmes rather than simply fitting as many places as possible into an itinerary. If something is impractical or would make the journey unnecessarily rushed, we will tell you and suggest a better alternative.
