Practical planning

We prefer to tell you what is workable.

A good itinerary should work on the ground, not only look good on paper. Traffic restrictions, temple procedures, river conditions, local events, drive times and crowd movement can all change how a day should be planned.

We therefore prefer realistic programmes rather than simply fitting as many places as possible into an itinerary. If something is impractical or would make the journey unnecessarily rushed, we will tell you and suggest a better alternative.
