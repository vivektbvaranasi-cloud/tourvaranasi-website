About Tour Varanasi

Private travel is only as good as the people making the journey work on the ground.

Based in Varanasi since 2016

We plan the city from inside it.

Tour Varanasi was established in 2016 as an independent, locally based specialist for private journeys through Varanasi and the surrounding cultural, sacred and Buddhist regions.

Varanasi is not an easy destination to experience well from a standard sightseeing list. Its most memorable moments often sit between the headline sights — on the river before the city fully wakes, inside neighbourhoods reached only on foot, in markets and food lanes, during living rituals, or in the explanation that helps a visitor understand what is happening around them.

2016

Locally rooted from the beginning.

Our role is not simply to put places into an itinerary. We think about how a day actually works: where vehicles can go, when walking is more appropriate, how river or temple conditions affect the plan, when guests need time to rest, and which cultural experiences genuinely add something to the visit.

Meet the team

The people behind your journey.

A small local team connects planning before arrival with welcome assistance, guiding and on-ground support while you are in the city.

Travel Planning & Reservations

Zafar Adeeb

Zafar looks after much of the planning before a guest arrives. He responds to enquiries, prepares itineraries, coordinates hotel reservations and brings the individual parts of a journey together. His role is to make sure the programme is practical, well timed and suited to the traveller’s interests before the on-ground team takes over.

Guest Arrival & Welcome Assistance

Abhishek Paul

Abhishek is often one of the first members of our team a guest meets in Varanasi. He receives travellers at the airport or railway station, assists with hotel check-in and explains the programme for the days ahead. That personal welcome helps first-time visitors settle into the city comfortably and know exactly what to expect.

Private Guide & Varanasi Specialist

Sujeet Anand

Sujeet is one of our principal guides and accompanies guests through Varanasi’s sightseeing and local experiences. He helps explain the history, rituals, neighbourhoods and everyday traditions behind what visitors are seeing, rather than simply reciting monument names and dates. He also adjusts the pace and route when local conditions or guest interests call for it.

Guest Support & Experience Coordination

Bhavna Singh

Bhavna stays connected with guests while their journey is underway. She helps with on-ground communication, follows up on daily arrangements, assists when something needs attention and works with the team to resolve issues quickly. She also collects guest feedback, helping us understand what worked well and where a future journey can be improved.

Private by Design

From arrival to departure

A real person at each stage of the journey.

Zafar helps shape the programme before arrival. Abhishek helps welcome guests into the city. Sujeet interprets Varanasi on the ground. Bhavna remains connected during the stay. Behind them is a wider network of drivers, boatmen and local specialists.

That continuity is particularly valuable for first-time international visitors to a city that can initially feel intense, complex and wonderfully unfamiliar.
