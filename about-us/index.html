Local knowledge is not a tagline. It is how the journey works.

We are a Varanasi-based private travel specialist planning the city from inside it — with people on the ground before you arrive and while you travel.

Based in Varanasi since 2016

We plan the city from inside it.

Varanasi rarely works well as a standard sightseeing checklist. What looks simple on a map can involve traffic restrictions, walking, changing temple access, river conditions and very different crowd levels through the day.

That is why our planning starts with how the city actually moves, not simply with a list of places. We think about the right start time, where a vehicle can realistically reach, when a walk makes more sense, how much rest a guest may need and which experiences genuinely add something to the journey.

2016

Tour Varanasi was established as an independent, locally based specialist for private journeys through Varanasi and the surrounding sacred, cultural and Buddhist regions.

Our job is not to make every day look full on paper. It is to make the day feel well planned when you are actually here.

What happens after you contact us?

A simple process, with people at every stage.

You do not need to know every sightseeing place before writing to us. A few practical details are enough for us to start shaping the right plan.

01

We understand your trip

Dates, flight or train timings, interests, walking comfort, hotel preference and whether you want full days or a slower pace all materially change the plan.

02

We build a realistic programme

We consider road movement, walking access, river timing, temple conditions and what should not be squeezed into the same day simply because it looks possible on a map.

03

Someone is there when you arrive

Arrival assistance and a clear explanation of the programme help first-time visitors settle into Varanasi more comfortably and know what to expect.

04

We stay connected while you travel

If local conditions change, the team can adjust arrangements here rather than leaving you to solve the problem from a distance.

A useful kind of honesty

What we will tell you — even when it is not what you hoped to hear.

Good local planning sometimes means saying no to an unrealistic sequence or explaining that an arrangement is subject to conditions. We would rather set the expectation properly before the day begins.

Sometimes boats do not operate.

Water level, weather and local administration instructions can affect river movement. We do not promise a boat ride when conditions do not allow one.

Temple access can change.

Security, festival dates and crowd-control procedures can alter access and priority arrangements. We plan around the rules that apply on the day.

Some places require more walking than expected.

The old city has narrow lanes and vehicle restrictions. We explain this beforehand and adjust routes for walking comfort wherever practical.

Trying to fit everything into one day can make Varanasi less enjoyable.

A well-paced itinerary often creates a better experience than adding one more temple or stop simply because there is space on the schedule.
